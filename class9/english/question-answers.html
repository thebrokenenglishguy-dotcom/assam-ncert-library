📘 Beehive — Textbook in English for Class IX

1. The Fun They Had

1. What did Margie write in her diary?
Answer: She wrote that Tommy had found a real book. She was surprised because the pages were old, yellow and had printed words.
2. Why did Margie hate school?
Answer: She had a mechanical teacher that gave her tests and homework. She especially disliked the geography lessons because the machine was giving her poor results.
3. Why did Margie think the old schools were more fun?
Answer: Children studied together in a school building, helped one another and had human teachers. Margie imagined that this made learning enjoyable.

2. The Sound of Music

1. How did Evelyn Glennie learn music despite being deaf?
Answer: She learned to sense music through vibrations in different parts of her body. Ron Forbes helped her discover how to feel the sounds.
2. Why is Bismillah Khan remembered?
Answer: He brought the shehnai to the classical music stage and became one of India's greatest shehnai players.

3. The Little Girl

1. Why was Kezia afraid of her father?
Answer: Her father spoke loudly, looked strict and expected discipline. Kezia felt nervous whenever she was near him.
2. What changed Kezia's opinion of her father?
Answer: When she was frightened during a nightmare, her father comforted her and let her sleep beside him. She understood that he loved her.

4. A Truly Beautiful Mind

1. Why was Einstein considered unusual as a child?
Answer: He began speaking late and was slow to learn. He disliked strict schooling and preferred independent thinking.
2. Why is Einstein's mind called beautiful?
Answer: He was a great scientist and also cared about peace, humanity and justice. His concern for people made his personality beautiful.

5. The Snake and the Mirror

1. What kind of person was the doctor?
Answer: He was young, unmarried, poor and rather proud of his appearance. He also dreamed of becoming rich.
2. What happened when the snake landed on the doctor?
Answer: The snake coiled around his arm and stared at itself in the mirror. The doctor stayed completely still until the snake moved away.

6. My Childhood

1. What values did Kalam learn from his parents?
Answer: He learned simplicity, honesty, kindness, faith and respect for people of different religions.
2. What happened when a new teacher objected to Kalam sitting with Ramanadha Sastry?
Answer: The teacher asked Kalam to sit at the back because of his religion. Ramanadha's father strongly opposed this discrimination and made the teacher understand the importance of equality.

7. Packing

1. Why does the author say he is good at packing?
Answer: He humorously claims that he knows how to pack well, but the events show that he creates confusion and makes packing much harder than necessary.
2. What makes the story humorous?
Answer: The narrator and his friends repeatedly make silly mistakes, forget things and blame one another. Their ordinary task becomes a comic situation.

8. Reach for the Top

1. What qualities helped Santosh Yadav become successful?
Answer: She was determined, courageous, disciplined and willing to challenge traditional expectations.
2. What made Maria Sharapova successful?
Answer: Strong determination, hard work, mental toughness and sacrifice helped her become a successful tennis player.

9. The Bond of Love

1. Why did Bruno become very close to the author and his family?
Answer: Bruno was raised by the family and received constant care and affection. He became emotionally attached to them.
2. Why was Bruno unhappy at the zoo?
Answer: He missed the family and was lonely. He became happy again when he was reunited with them.

10. Kathmandu

1. What did the author observe in Kathmandu?
Answer: He observed busy streets, temples, worshippers, shops, vendors and many different sights and sounds.
2. How did the flute seller attract the author's attention?
Answer: The soft and sweet sound of the flute stood out among the noisy sounds of the city.

11. If I Were You

1. How did Gerrard save himself from the intruder?
Answer: Gerrard used his intelligence and presence of mind. He convinced the intruder that he was a criminal who was already wanted by the police and made him enter the cupboard.
2. What qualities does Gerrard show?
Answer: He is calm, clever, confident and quick-thinking in a dangerous situation.

12. A Visit to Kaziranga and Sivasagar

1. Why are Kaziranga and Sivasagar important?
Answer: Kaziranga is famous for its wildlife, especially the one-horned rhinoceros. Sivasagar is important for Assam's Ahom history and monuments.
2. What can students learn from visiting such places?
Answer: They can learn about Assam's natural environment, wildlife, history and cultural heritage through direct observation.

The Road Not Taken — Poem

1. What do the two roads represent?
Answer: They represent different choices in life. The traveller must choose one path and leave the other.
2. What is the main idea of the poem?
Answer: Life involves choices, and our decisions can shape our future.

Wind — Poem

1. What does the wind symbolise?
Answer: The wind symbolises difficulties and challenges in life.
2. What does the poet advise us to do?
Answer: We should make ourselves strong enough to face difficulties instead of being broken by them.

Rain on the Roof — Poem

1. What happens when the poet hears the rain?
Answer: The sound of rain creates pleasant memories and brings thoughts of his childhood and his mother.

The Lake Isle of Innisfree — Poem

1. Why does the poet want to go to Innisfree?
Answer: He wants a peaceful life close to nature, away from the noise and pressure of the city.

A Legend of the Northland — Poem

1. Why was the woman changed into a woodpecker?
Answer: She was selfish and refused to give a small cake to Saint Peter. As punishment for her greed, she was changed into a bird that must search for food.

No Men Are Foreign — Poem

1. What message does the poem give?
Answer: All human beings are equal. We should not hate people because of their country, race or appearance.

The Duck and the Kangaroo — Poem

1. Why does the Duck want to travel?
Answer: The Duck is tired of staying in one place and wants to see the world.

On Killing a Tree — Poem

1. Why is a tree difficult to kill?
Answer: A tree has deep roots and can grow again. Cutting its branches is not enough; its roots must also be destroyed.

Trees — Poem

1. What do the trees symbolise?
Answer: The trees can symbolise freedom, growth and the desire to escape restrictions.

The Snake Trying — Poem

1. What does the poet want us to understand about the snake?
Answer: The snake is trying to escape and does not intend to harm anyone. We should not hurt creatures unnecessarily.

A Slumber Did My Spirit Seal — Poem

1. What change has happened to the poet's beloved?
Answer: She has died and become part of nature. The poet accepts that she is now beyond ordinary human concerns.

📚 Moments — Supplementary Reader for Class IX

1. The Lost Child

1. Why does the child refuse the things he wanted earlier after getting lost?
Answer: Once he loses his parents, he becomes frightened and wants only their presence. Sweets, toys and other attractions no longer matter.
2. What is the main message of the story?
Answer: The love and security of parents are more valuable to a child than material attractions.

2. The Adventures of Toto

1. Why did Grandfather buy Toto?
Answer: Grandfather liked animals and found Toto amusing. He bought the monkey from a tonga-driver.
2. Why could Toto not remain in the house?
Answer: He was mischievous and destructive. He damaged clothes, dishes and other household things.

3. Iswaran the Storyteller

1. In what way was Iswaran an asset to Mahendra?
Answer: Iswaran cooked, cleaned and looked after Mahendra wherever he was posted. His lively stories also kept Mahendra entertained.
2. What happened to Mahendra on a full-moon night?
Answer: He saw a dark, ghost-like figure outside and became frightened. The experience made him decide to leave the place.

4. In the Kingdom of Fools

1. Why was the kingdom called a kingdom of fools?
Answer: The king and his minister made absurd rules. They ordered people to work at night and sleep during the day and judged cases without logic.
2. How did the disciple save himself?
Answer: He cleverly used the king's greed and fear. By claiming that the first person to die would go straight to heaven, he made the king choose himself for execution.

5. The Happy Prince

1. Why did the Prince become unhappy after death?
Answer: From his statue he could see the poverty and suffering in the city. He realised that people were unhappy while he had lived in comfort.
2. Why did the Swallow stay with the Prince?
Answer: The Swallow was moved by the Prince's kindness and helped distribute the jewels and gold to poor people.

6. Weathering the Storm in Ersama

1. How did Prashant help the people after the cyclone?
Answer: He organised young people, arranged food and shelter, helped clean the village and worked for the rehabilitation of victims.
2. What qualities of Prashant stand out?
Answer: He shows courage, leadership, compassion and a strong sense of responsibility.

7. The Last Leaf

1. What does Johnsy believe about the last ivy leaf?
Answer: She believes that she will die when the last leaf falls.
2. What was Behrman's masterpiece?
Answer: He painted a realistic ivy leaf on the wall during a storm. Johnsy believed it was a real leaf and regained hope.

8. A House Is Not a Home

1. Why was the narrator unhappy at his new school?
Answer: He felt lonely and missed his old friends. He also felt uncomfortable because he did not know the other students.
2. How did the narrator's situation improve?
Answer: His classmates accepted him, and he was reunited with his pet cat. He learned that friendship and love make a place feel like home.

9. The Accidental Tourist

1. Why does Bill Bryson call himself an accidental tourist?
Answer: He often travels but is clumsy and gets into embarrassing situations while travelling.
2. What makes the story humorous?
Answer: The narrator describes his own mistakes honestly, especially his problems with food, luggage and travel arrangements.

10. The Beggar

1. How does Sergei help Lushkoff?
Answer: Sergei refuses to encourage his lies and offers him work. This gives Lushkoff a chance to change his life.
2. Who is mainly responsible for Lushkoff's change?
Answer: Olga plays the greatest emotional role. Her kindness and silent suffering touch Lushkoff and help him become honest.
