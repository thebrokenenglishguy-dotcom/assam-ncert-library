1. Important Career Options in Information Technology

The chapter explains that Information Technology offers many career opportunities. Important options shown in the case study are:

  • Support Specialist
  • Web Developer
  • System Analyst
  • Database Administrator
  • IT Coordinator
  • Computer Programmer
  • IT Technician
  • Network Engineer
  • Web Administrator
  • Data Entry Operator
Case-study question: If you were Swadesh, which IT career option would you choose and why?
Example answer: I would choose Web Developer because I am interested in creating websites and solving problems using technology. It is a useful career with opportunities to learn new technologies.

2. Making Your Biodata in MS Word

Case study: Sameer Ali has passed Class 12 and is looking for a full-time job. A job advertisement asks candidates to prepare their bio-data and send it as an attachment.

What software should Sameer use to create his biodata?
He can use MS Word to create a neat and professional biodata/resume. It can then be saved and attached to an email.
Important points to include in a biodata
Name and contact details, career objective, educational qualification, skills, experience if any, achievements, personal details and declaration can be included.

3. Creating a Brochure for Your School in MS Word

Case study: A school is organising a virtual alumni meet. Students are asked to create a brochure in MS Word for the upcoming event.

What is the purpose of the brochure?
A brochure presents important information about an event in an attractive and organised form. It may include the event name, date, time, venue or online link, programme details, contact information and suitable images.

4. Creating a Report Card in MS Excel

Case study: Teachers are asked to prepare the final-session report card of Grade IX using MS Excel.

Why is MS Excel useful for preparing a report card?
Excel allows marks to be entered in rows and columns and formulas can automatically calculate totals, averages, percentages and grades. This makes the report card easier and faster to prepare.

5. Creating a Presentation in MS PowerPoint with Biography of Great Personalities of India

Case study: A student is asked to prepare a PowerPoint presentation about a great Indian personality who inspires them.

What can the presentation contain?
It can include the person's name, early life, education, major achievements, contribution to India, important events, pictures, inspiring qualities and a conclusion about why the person is an inspiration.
Why is PowerPoint suitable for this task?
PowerPoint allows information to be presented through slides using text, pictures, designs, animations and other visual elements. This makes a biography more engaging and easy to understand.
