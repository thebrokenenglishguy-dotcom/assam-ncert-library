3. Answer the Following

1. What are the main functions related to information?
The main functions related to information are collection, storage, processing, retrieval, transmission and distribution of information.
2. Define the term plagiarism.
Plagiarism is the act of copying another person's words, ideas or work and presenting them as one's own without proper acknowledgement.
3. What is software piracy? What are its common forms?
Software piracy is the unauthorized copying, use or distribution of copyrighted software. Common forms include softlifting, hard-disk loading, counterfeiting and illegal electronic distribution.
4. Write down the major issues of security and integrity of information.
Major issues include unauthorized access, theft of information, alteration or destruction of data, malware and viruses, privacy violations, and unauthorized disclosure of information.
5. Name three types of law that can help restrict software piracy.
Three types are copyright law, patent law and trademark law.
6. An extended synonym for Information Technology is ICT. So, how ICT differs from IT?
IT mainly deals with computers, software and information systems. ICT (Information and Communication Technology) includes IT as well as communication technologies such as telecommunications, networks and the Internet.
7. IT security today has become a major point of concern. What comes under IT security?
IT security includes protecting computers, networks, software and data from unauthorized access, misuse, damage, malware and other threats. It involves measures such as passwords, access control, antivirus software, encryption and backups.
8. Elaborate the term software ethics.
Software ethics means following responsible and lawful practices while creating, using, copying or distributing software. It includes respecting copyrights and licences, avoiding piracy, protecting privacy and using software honestly.
9. Explain the need of protecting intellectual property right.
Intellectual property rights protect the original work and ideas of creators. They prevent unauthorized copying or use, give creators legal ownership and encourage people to create new software, inventions and other works.
