3. Short Answer Questions

1. What is topology? From your understanding describe which topology will be best to set up a computer network in your school.
Topology is the physical or logical arrangement of computers and other devices in a network. For a school, star topology is generally suitable because each computer connects to a central switch, making the network easier to manage and troubleshoot.
2. Give an example of a URL and describe different sections of it.
Example: https://www.example.com/index.html. https is the protocol, www is the web-service prefix, example.com is the domain name, and /index.html identifies the particular webpage or resource.
3. What is ranking? How is it related to a search engine?
Ranking is the order in which search results are displayed. Search engines rank webpages according to factors such as relevance and usefulness so that more relevant results appear higher.
4. What is the use of BCC in an email? Describe with an example.
BCC (Blind Carbon Copy) sends a copy of an email to recipients without showing their email addresses to the other recipients. For example, a teacher can send the same notice to many parents using BCC so that the recipients cannot see one another's email addresses.
5. What are the differences between Chat and Video Conferencing?
Chat mainly allows people to communicate through typed messages, while video conferencing allows people to communicate using live video and usually audio. Video conferencing is more suitable for virtual meetings and classes because participants can see and hear each other.
