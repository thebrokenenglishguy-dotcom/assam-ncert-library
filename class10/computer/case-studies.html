Case Study 1

Deep is a medical student. He needs to prepare a thesis document and a presentation for seminars. He also needs cloud storage, a calendar and a map application.

a. Where would he create the document for his thesis?

He can use Google Docs or Microsoft Word Online to create his thesis document.

b. Where would he create the presentation for the seminar?

He can use Google Slides or Microsoft PowerPoint to create his presentation.

c. What is the benefit of using such platform?

  1. He can access his work from any computer or mobile.
  2. The document is saved online.
  3. He can edit the file from different places.
  4. He can easily share the document or presentation with others.
  5. There is less risk of losing the file if the computer stops working.

d. Where would he keep the documents so that he can avail the documents from anywhere (if USB is not working)?

He can store the documents in cloud storage such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. He can then access the documents using the Internet.

e. Where he schedules the seminar and gets reminders and why?

He can use Google Calendar. It allows him to schedule the seminar, set the date and time, add the seminar location, and set reminders so that he does not forget the seminar.

f. On the way to catch his flight to the airport, he lost his way. Suggest an App that Deep can use to find the location of the airport.

He can use Google Maps. It can show his current location, find the airport, show the route and give directions to the airport.

Case Study 2

Rohan wants to take his parents for Char Dham Yatra. He wants to book tickets and hotels online.

a. What is the advantage of online ticketing?

  1. Tickets can be booked from home.
  2. It saves time.
  3. There is no need to stand in a long queue.
  4. We can check available seats before booking.
  5. Payment can be made online.
  6. The ticket can be stored on a mobile phone.

b. Why is online booking important?

  • It makes booking quick and easy.
  • We can book tickets from anywhere.
  • We can check availability immediately.
  • It is useful when there is a large rush.
  • It helps in planning the journey in advance.

c. What are the disadvantages of booking online?

  1. Internet connection is required.
  2. Online fraud can happen.
  3. Fake websites may steal money or personal information.
  4. Technical problems may occur during payment.
  5. Some people may find online booking difficult.

d. What are the requirements of online booking?

  1. A mobile phone or computer.
  2. Internet connection.
  3. An account on the booking website/app.
  4. Basic personal details.
  5. A valid mobile number or email ID.
  6. An online payment method such as UPI, debit card or credit card.

e. He is confused which websites to search for booking online tickets. Can you help him with some website names?

  • IRCTC – for railway tickets.
  • MakeMyTrip – for flights, trains and hotels.
  • Yatra – for travel bookings.
  • Booking.com – for hotels and accommodation.
  • redBus – for bus tickets.

He should always check that he is using the official and genuine website/app.

Case Study 3

Nabanita wants to buy goods from home using e-commerce.

a. What is the difference between traditional shopping and e-commerce?

Traditional ShoppingE-commerce
We visit a physical shop.We buy products through a website or app.
We have to travel to the shop.We can shop from home.
Payment can be made at the shop.Payment can be made online or on delivery.
We get the product immediately in most cases.The product is usually delivered later.
Shop timings may be limited.Online stores are generally available 24×7.

b. What are the advantages of e-commerce?

  1. We can shop from home.
  2. It saves time and travelling.
  3. Many products are available in one place.
  4. We can compare prices.
  5. Customer reviews can help us choose products.
  6. Products can be delivered to our home.

c. Which are the best e-commerce sites?

Some popular e-commerce websites are Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and Meesho. The best website depends on the product, price, delivery and reliability.

d. What are the possible risks of e-commerce websites?

  1. Fake websites may cheat customers.
  2. Fake or poor-quality products may be sold.
  3. Personal information may be stolen.
  4. Payment fraud may occur.
  5. Products may be damaged or different from what was ordered.

e. What are the risks of online transactions?

  • Online payment fraud.
  • Phishing attacks.
  • Theft of passwords or OTPs.
  • Fake payment links.
  • Unauthorized access to bank accounts.
Safety rule: Never share your OTP, PIN, CVV or banking password with anyone.

Case Study 4

Aditya is a freelancer and wants to learn about blogging.

a. What exactly is a blog?

A blog is a website or part of a website where a person regularly publishes articles, information, opinions or experiences.

b. How does she start a blog?

  1. Choosing a topic.
  2. Choosing a blogging platform such as WordPress or Blogger.
  3. Creating an account.
  4. Choosing a blog name.
  5. Writing useful articles.
  6. Publishing the articles.

c. Do bloggers make money?

Yes. Bloggers can earn money through:
  1. Advertisements.
  2. Affiliate marketing.
  3. Sponsored posts.
  4. Selling their own products or services.
  5. Paid memberships or subscriptions.

d. What are the different types of blogs?

  1. Educational blogs
  2. Travel blogs
  3. Food blogs
  4. Technology blogs
  5. Fashion blogs
  6. Health and fitness blogs
  7. Personal blogs
  8. News blogs

e. How did she choose a topic for her blog?

She should choose a topic in which she has knowledge or interest, which people are interested in reading, about which she can regularly create useful content, and which matches her skills and experience.

f. How can she be a successful blogger?

  1. Write useful and original content.
  2. Post regularly.
  3. Use simple and clear language.
  4. Use suitable images.
  5. Avoid copying other people's content.
  6. Learn basic SEO.
  7. Promote her blog on social media.
  8. Understand what her readers want.
  9. Be patient and consistent.

Case Study 5

Roshmi has completed graduation and started working in an IT firm. She wants to improve her academic skills but cannot afford expensive courses.

Complete guidance for Roshmi

1. Use online courses

She can learn through platforms such as SWAYAM, NPTEL, Coursera, edX and YouTube educational channels.

2. Learn according to her job

She should first identify the skills needed for her promotion, such as programming, data analysis, communication, computer skills and project management.

3. Use free study materials

She can use e-books, online notes, video lectures, practice exercises and online tutorials.

4. Take certification courses

She can complete free or low-cost certificate courses related to her job.

5. Practise regularly

She should give some time every day to learning and practising new skills.

6. Learn from her workplace

She can ask experienced colleagues for guidance, take part in projects and learn new tools used in the company.

7. Build practical skills

She should not only study theory. She should also work on small projects to improve her practical knowledge.

8. Make a learning plan

1 hour daily → Learning
30 minutes → Practice
Weekend → Revision/project

Conclusion

Roshmi does not need to spend a lot of money. With free online resources, regular practice and proper planning, she can improve her skills and increase her chances of promotion.
