Management of Natural Resources

Class X Science · Assam textbook

Questions inside the chapter

1. Why should natural resources be managed sustainably?
Answer: Resources are limited and must support present needs without damaging the ability of future generations to meet theirs.
2. What is sustainable development?
Answer: Development that meets present needs while conserving resources and environmental quality for the future.
3. Why is the 3R principle important?
Answer: Reduce, Reuse and Recycle lower resource use, waste generation and pollution.
4. Why is community participation important in resource management?
Answer: Local communities depend directly on resources and can help conserve, monitor and use them responsibly.

End-of-chapter Exercises

1. What is sustainable management of forests?
Answer: Using forest resources while conserving biodiversity, soil, water and the ability of forests to regenerate.
2. Why should coal and petroleum be conserved?
Answer: They are finite, non-renewable resources and their combustion causes pollution and greenhouse-gas emissions.
3. What is rainwater harvesting?
Answer: Collecting and storing rainwater, often from rooftops or catchment areas, for later use or groundwater recharge.
4. Why are dams both useful and controversial?
Answer: They provide irrigation, drinking water, electricity and flood control, but can displace people, submerge habitats and alter river ecosystems.
5. Why should wildlife be protected?
Answer: Wildlife maintains biodiversity and ecosystem functions, and species loss can destabilise ecosystems.
6. What is the Chipko-type approach to forest conservation?
Answer: Local people protect trees and forests from destructive cutting and demand sustainable use of forest resources.
7. Why should water resources be conserved?
Answer: Fresh water is limited and essential for life, agriculture, industry and ecosystems.
8. How does the 3R approach reduce pressure on resources?
Answer: Reducing lowers consumption, reusing extends product life, and recycling recovers materials.
9. Why is equitable sharing of resources important?
Answer: It prevents overuse by a few groups and helps ensure fair access while protecting the resource base.
10. What is a major goal of natural-resource management?
Answer: To balance human needs, economic development, conservation and the rights of future generations.
