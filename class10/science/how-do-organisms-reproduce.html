How do Organisms Reproduce?

Class X Science · Assam textbook

Questions inside the chapter

1. Why is DNA copying essential in reproduction?
Answer: It transfers hereditary information to the next generation and helps create a new individual.
2. Why are variations important?
Answer: They may help some individuals survive environmental changes and support continuation of a species.
3. How does binary fission differ from multiple fission?
Answer: Binary fission produces two daughter cells; multiple fission produces many daughter cells from one parent cell.
4. Why is sexual reproduction useful?
Answer: It combines genetic material from two parents and creates greater variation.

End-of-chapter Exercises

1. Asexual reproduction through budding occurs in which organism?
Answer: Yeast.
2. Which is not part of the female reproductive system: ovary, uterus, vas deferens, fallopian tube?
Answer: Vas deferens.
3. What does the anther contain?
Answer: Pollen grains.
4. Give advantages of sexual reproduction over asexual reproduction.
Answer: It produces greater variation, which can improve adaptation and survival of populations.
5. What are the functions of testes?
Answer: They produce sperm and secrete the male sex hormone testosterone.
6. Why does menstruation occur?
Answer: If fertilisation does not occur, the thickened uterine lining breaks down and is discharged.
7. What should a labelled longitudinal flower diagram show?
Answer: Sepals, petals, anther, filament, stigma, style, ovary and ovules, as appropriate.
8. What are major methods of contraception?
Answer: Barrier methods, hormonal methods, intrauterine devices, surgical methods and natural methods.
9. How does reproduction differ in unicellular and multicellular organisms?
Answer: In unicellular organisms, reproduction may be by simple cell division. Multicellular organisms use specialised reproductive cells/organs and more complex processes.
10. How does reproduction provide stability to species?
Answer: It produces new individuals and maintains the population while passing hereditary information to the next generation.
11. Why might people adopt contraception?
Answer: To avoid unwanted pregnancy, space children, control family size and, for condoms, reduce the risk of some sexually transmitted infections.
