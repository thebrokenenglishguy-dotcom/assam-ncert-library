Our Environment

Class X Science · Assam textbook

Questions inside the chapter

1. What is an ecosystem?
Answer: A system in which living organisms interact with one another and with non-living components of their surroundings.
2. What is a food chain?
Answer: A sequence showing transfer of food and energy from producers through consumers.
3. What is biological magnification?
Answer: The increasing concentration of persistent harmful chemicals at higher trophic levels.
4. Why is ozone important?
Answer: The ozone layer absorbs much of the Sun’s harmful ultraviolet radiation.

End-of-chapter Exercises

1. Which group contains only biodegradable items?
Answer: Grass, flowers and leather; also cake, wood and grass are biodegradable, but the single-best MCQ option is the group containing only biodegradable items.
2. Which set forms a food chain?
Answer: Grass → goat → human.
3. Which practice is environment-friendly?
Answer: Carrying cloth bags, switching off unnecessary electrical devices and walking when practical are all environment-friendly.
4. What happens if all organisms at one trophic level are removed?
Answer: The food web is disturbed, causing population changes at other levels and possible ecosystem instability.
5. Would removing organisms from different trophic levels have the same effect?
Answer: No. The effect depends on the role and position of the trophic level; removing producers is especially severe.
6. What is biological magnification and why can it differ across trophic levels?
Answer: Persistent toxins become concentrated as organisms at higher levels consume many contaminated organisms, so higher trophic levels often have greater concentrations.
7. What problems are caused by non-biodegradable waste?
Answer: It persists, accumulates, pollutes land and water, harms wildlife and can enter food chains.
8. Would an entirely biodegradable waste stream have no environmental impact?
Answer: No. Large amounts can still consume oxygen, produce odours, spread pathogens and overload decomposition systems.
9. Why is ozone-layer damage a concern and how can it be reduced?
Answer: More UV radiation can reach Earth and harm living organisms. Limiting ozone-depleting substances such as CFCs helps protect the layer.
