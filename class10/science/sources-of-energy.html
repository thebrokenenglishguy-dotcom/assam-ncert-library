Sources of Energy

Class X Science · Assam textbook

Questions inside the chapter

1. What makes a good source of energy?
Answer: It should provide large energy output, be accessible, easy to store/transport, economical and preferably cause little environmental harm.
2. What makes a good fuel?
Answer: It should have high calorific value, be easily available, economical, easy to store/transport and burn cleanly.
3. Why are fossil fuels called non-renewable?
Answer: They form over millions of years and are consumed much faster than they are naturally replaced.
4. Why can no energy source be perfectly ideal?
Answer: Every source has limitations such as cost, availability, efficiency, pollution or environmental impact.

End-of-chapter Exercises

1. Can a solar water heater work on a cloudy day as effectively as a sunny day?
Answer: No. It needs sufficient solar radiation, so a cloudy day reduces heating.
2. Which is not a biomass source: wood, gobar gas, nuclear energy or coal?
Answer: Nuclear energy.
3. Which is not ultimately derived from solar energy: geothermal, wind, nuclear or biomass?
Answer: Nuclear energy.
4. Compare fossil fuels and the Sun as direct energy sources.
Answer: Fossil fuels are concentrated but finite and polluting. Solar energy is renewable and cleaner but intermittent and diffuse.
5. Compare biomass and hydroelectricity.
Answer: Biomass is chemical energy stored in organic matter and can be used as fuel; hydroelectricity uses falling/flowing water to generate electricity and needs suitable geography.
6. Give limitations of wind, wave and tidal energy.
Answer: Wind is intermittent and needs suitable sites; wave energy depends on suitable coastlines; tidal energy requires large tidal ranges and suitable locations.
7. Differentiate renewable/non-renewable and exhaustible/inexhaustible.
Answer: Renewable sources are naturally replenished; non-renewable are replenished very slowly. Exhaustible sources can be depleted; inexhaustible sources are available on human time scales. The classifications overlap but are not identical.
8. What qualities should an ideal energy source have?
Answer: High energy density, easy availability, low cost, easy storage/transport, reliable supply and low pollution.
9. Give advantages and disadvantages of solar cookers.
Answer: Advantages: clean, free solar energy and no fuel consumption. Disadvantages: depend on sunlight and are less useful at night, in heavy clouds or rainy weather.
10. What environmental problems arise from increasing energy demand?
Answer: More mining, air pollution, greenhouse gases, habitat damage, waste and climate impacts can result. Efficiency and renewable energy can reduce these impacts.
