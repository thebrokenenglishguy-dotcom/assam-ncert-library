Exercise — Very Short Answer Questions

1. Why was the UNO formed?

  • The United Nations Organization (UNO) was formed to maintain international peace and security.
  • It was also formed to prevent future wars and promote cooperation among countries. United Nations

2. Mention two objectives of the UNO.

  1. To maintain international peace and security.
  2. To promote friendly relations and cooperation among nations. United Nations

3. Write the names of the UN organs.

The six principal organs of the UN are:

  1. General Assembly
  2. Security Council
  3. Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC)
  4. International Court of Justice (ICJ)
  5. Secretariat
  6. Trusteeship Council
United Nations

4. Write the names of the two Agencies related to the UNO.

  • UNESCO – United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization
  • WHO – World Health Organization

5. Write the names of two Treaties which were signed on Disarmament.

  • Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT)
  • Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty (CTBT)

6. What is meant by Human Rights?

  • Human Rights are the basic rights and freedoms that every person has simply because they are human.
  • They include rights such as life, liberty, equality and dignity. United Nations

Long Answer Questions

1. Discuss the aims of the UNO for which it came into existence and also discuss its principles.

The United Nations Organization (UNO) was established in 1945 after the Second World War. Its main aim was to maintain peace and promote cooperation among countries.

Aims of the UNO:

  1. To maintain world peace – It works to prevent wars and conflicts between countries.
  2. To develop friendly relations – It promotes friendly relations among nations.
  3. To promote international cooperation – Countries cooperate in economic, social, cultural and humanitarian matters.
  4. To protect human rights – It promotes respect for human rights and basic freedoms.
  5. To solve international problems – It provides a platform for countries to discuss and solve common problems.
  6. To promote development – It works for better living conditions and social and economic development. United Nations

Principles of the UNO:

  1. All member countries are sovereign and equal.
  2. Countries should settle disputes peacefully.
  3. Countries should not use force or threat against other countries.
  4. Member countries should follow the UN Charter.
  5. Countries should cooperate with the UNO in maintaining peace.
  6. The UNO should generally respect the internal affairs of countries. United Nations Legal Affairs

2. Write a note on the Security Council of the UNO.

The Security Council is one of the six principal organs of the UNO. Its main responsibility is to maintain international peace and security. United Nations

  1. The Security Council has 15 members.
  2. It has 5 permanent members: China, France, Russia, United Kingdom and United States.
  3. There are 10 non-permanent members.
  4. Non-permanent members are elected for two-year terms. UN Security Council
  5. It investigates situations that may cause international conflict.
  6. It encourages countries to settle disputes peacefully.
  7. It can impose sanctions in certain situations.
  8. In serious cases, it can authorize the use of military force. UN Security Council

In short: The Security Council plays an important role in maintaining international peace and security.

3. Discuss the steps taken by the UNO on world peace.

  1. Peaceful settlement of disputes – It encourages countries to solve disputes through discussion and negotiation.
  2. Peacekeeping operations – UN peacekeeping forces are sent to conflict areas to help maintain peace.
  3. Prevention of wars – The UNO investigates situations that may lead to war.
  4. Sanctions – The Security Council may impose economic or other sanctions against countries involved in serious threats to peace.
  5. Disarmament – The UNO encourages countries to reduce dangerous weapons.
  6. International cooperation – It brings countries together to discuss international problems.
  7. Protection of human rights – Respect for human rights helps promote peace and security.
  8. International law – The UNO supports peaceful settlement of disputes according to international law. United Nations

4. Write a note regarding National Human Rights Commission in India.

The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) is an important institution for the protection and promotion of human rights in India.

  1. The NHRC was established on 12 October 1993.
  2. It was established under the Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993. NHRC
  3. It works to protect the life, liberty, equality and dignity of people.
  4. It can inquire into complaints of human rights violations.
  5. It can investigate cases of alleged human rights violations.
  6. It spreads awareness about human rights.
  7. It can make recommendations to the government regarding human rights protection.
  8. It also works for better awareness and respect for human rights in India. NHRC

5. Discuss the steps taken by the UNO regarding Human Rights.

  1. Universal Declaration of Human Rights – In 1948, the UN adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR).
  2. Equality – It recognizes that all human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights.
  3. Right to life and liberty – It supports the right to life, freedom and personal security.
  4. Protection from discrimination – It promotes equal rights without discrimination based on race, sex, language or religion.
  5. Freedom – It supports freedom of thought, belief, expression and religion.
  6. Protection from torture – It opposes torture and cruel or inhuman treatment.
  7. Right to education and other basic needs – The UN promotes social and economic rights and better living conditions.
  8. International cooperation – The UN encourages countries to work together to protect human rights.

The UDHR became an important international document for the protection of human rights. United Nations
