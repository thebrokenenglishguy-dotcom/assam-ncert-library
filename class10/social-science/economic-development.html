Exercise — Very Short Answer Type Questions

Answer in one sentence

1. Economic growth

Answer:

Economic growth means an increase in the production of goods and services and per capita income of a country.

2. Economic development

Answer:

Economic development means economic growth along with improvement in the standard of living and social conditions of people.

3. Human development

Answer:

Human development means the expansion of the choices and opportunities available to people.

4. Economic planning

Answer:

Economic planning is a method of achieving well-defined economic objectives within a fixed period through planned efforts.

5. Democratic planning

Answer:

Democratic planning is planning in which people participate in different stages of making and implementing plans.

6. Liberalisation

Answer:

Liberalisation means reducing government controls and restrictions on economic activities.

7. Privatisation

Answer:

Privatisation means increasing the participation of the private sector in activities previously dominated by the public sector.

8. Globalisation

Answer:

Globalisation means the integration of a country's economy with the economies of other countries.

Short Answer Type Questions

1. Mention two important points of difference between economic growth and economic development.

Economic GrowthEconomic Development
1. It is mainly a quantitative concept.1. It is a broader concept including both quantitative and qualitative changes.
2. It mainly deals with increase in output and income.2. It also includes improvement in living standards and social conditions.

In short: Economic growth is mainly about more income, while economic development is about better life along with economic growth.

2. What are the three indices of human development?

  1. Life expectancy – how long people are expected to live.
  2. Literacy/Education – the level of education of people.
  3. Standard of living – access to basic needs such as clean water and sanitation.

3. What is meant by extension of collective choice?

  • Extension of collective choice means giving people more opportunities and choices in social, economic and political matters.
  • It also promotes decentralisation of power.
  • In India, local bodies such as Panchayats and Municipalities allow people to participate in planning at the grassroots level.

4. Mention four important objectives of India's Five Year Plans.

  1. To increase the rate of economic growth.
  2. To make proper use of modern technology.
  3. To achieve self-reliance.
  4. To promote social justice and reduce inequalities.

6. Explain one of the main points of difference between the first phase of planning in India (1951-52 to 1990-91) and the second phase (1991-92 to the present day).

The main difference was the role of the public and private sectors.

First phase (1951-52 to 1990-91):

  • The public sector was the leading sector.
  • The private sector had a comparatively smaller role.

Second phase (1991-92 onwards):

  • Liberalisation, Privatisation and Globalisation (LPG) became important.
  • The role of the private sector increased.
  • The relative importance of the public sector decreased.

7. Briefly explain the three main causes of the introduction of economic reform measures in India.

1. Fiscal deficit

  • Government expenditure became higher than government revenue.
  • Many public sector enterprises were making losses.

2. High inflation

  • Prices of goods increased rapidly.
  • Poor and middle-income people were badly affected.

3. Balance of payments problem

  • India's foreign exchange position became very weak.
  • There was difficulty in meeting the country's international payment obligations.

These problems led India to introduce major economic reforms in 1991.

8. Mention three benefits of economic reforms in India.

  1. The rate of economic growth increased.
  2. The rate of price rise showed improvement in some periods.
  3. India's foreign exchange reserves increased considerably.

9. Briefly explain two problems of economic reforms in India.

1. Unequal distribution of benefits

  • The benefits of economic reforms were not received equally by everyone.
  • Urban and richer sections benefited more than many poorer sections.

2. Excessive consumerism

  • Economic reforms increased competition and consumer choices.
  • Excessive consumerism may weaken some social values.

10. Mention five important objectives of Assam's Twelfth Five Year Plan.

  1. To increase the growth rate of Assam's economy to 10% in the next two to three decades.
  2. To introduce schemes for poverty removal.
  3. To solve the problems of flood and erosion using modern technology and scientific management.
  4. To increase the annual growth rate of agriculture from 6% to 8%.
  5. To achieve self-sufficiency in power generation and use modern technology in power supply and distribution.

Easy way to remember: 10% Growth → Poverty → Flood → Agriculture → Power
