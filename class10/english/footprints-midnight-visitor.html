Questions & Answers

“Ausable did not fit any description of a secret agent Fowler had ever read.” What is unusual about Ausable?
Answer: Ausable is fat and has an ordinary appearance. He does not look like the glamorous secret agents Fowler expected. Yet he is intelligent, calm and highly capable.
How does Ausable manage to make Max believe that there is a balcony attached to his room?
Answer: Ausable invents a believable story about a balcony and says that someone often enters through it. He speaks confidently, so Max accepts the story.
When do you think Ausable thought up his plan for getting rid of Max?
Answer: He appears to create the plan during the crisis. He quickly uses the supposed balcony and the expected arrival of the police to make Max panic.
Who is Fowler and what is his first authentic thrill of the day?
Answer: Fowler is a young writer who has come to meet Ausable. His first real thrill comes when he witnesses the dangerous encounter with Max and sees Ausable handle it.
