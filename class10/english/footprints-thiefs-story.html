Questions & Answers

What are Hari Singh’s reactions to the prospect of receiving an education? Do they change over time? What makes him return to Anil?
Answer: At first Hari sees education mainly as a way to earn more money. Later he understands that education can make him a respected and capable person. Anil’s trust and kindness also make him feel that changing his life is possible, so he returns.
Why does not Anil hand the thief over to the police? In what ways is Anil different from such employers?
Answer: Anil understands that Hari has made a mistake and gives him another chance. He is trusting, kind and willing to teach Hari. He wants to reform him rather than simply punish him.
Who does ‘I’ refer to in the story?
Answer: ‘I’ refers to Hari Singh, the fifteen-year-old narrator and thief.
What does Hari get from Anil in return for his work?
Answer: Hari gets food and a place to stay. Anil also begins teaching him to read, write and do basic arithmetic.
