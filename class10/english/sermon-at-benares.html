Questions & Answers

1. Why does Kisa Gotami go to the Buddha?
Answer: Her only son has died. She is unable to accept his death and asks the Buddha for medicine to bring him back to life.
2. What does the Buddha ask her to bring?
Answer: He asks her to bring mustard seeds from a house where nobody has ever lost a family member.
3. What does Kisa Gotami learn from her search?
Answer: She discovers that death has touched every family. She understands that death is a universal part of human life.
4. What is the Buddha’s main teaching?
Answer: He teaches that people must accept the truth of death and overcome attachment and excessive grief.
5. Why is acceptance important?
Answer: Acceptance helps a person understand reality and live with greater peace instead of remaining trapped in sorrow.

Poem — For Anne Gregory

1. What does the poem say about outward beauty?
Answer: Outward beauty can attract human love, but true and lasting love is shown as deeper than physical appearance.
2. What does the final idea suggest?
Answer: The poem suggests that divine love is based on the inner self rather than external beauty.
