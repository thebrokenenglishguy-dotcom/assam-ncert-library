Questions & Answers

Noodle avoids offending Think-Tank but at the same time corrects his mistakes. How does he manage to do that?
Answer: Noodle speaks respectfully and carefully. He presents his corrections as gentle suggestions rather than openly challenging Think-Tank, allowing Think-Tank to save face.
If you were in Noodle’s place, how would you handle Think-Tank’s mistakes?
Answer: I would remain respectful but clearly provide the correct information. I would avoid embarrassing him and would use evidence to explain the mistake.
Do you think books are being replaced by electronic media? Can we do away with books altogether?
Answer: Electronic media is very useful, but books still provide reliable, focused and lasting information. Therefore, electronic media may change how we read, but books are unlikely to become completely unnecessary.
Why are books referred to as a man’s best companion?
Answer: Books give knowledge, ideas, entertainment and guidance. They can be used repeatedly and remain available whenever a reader needs them.
