Questions & Answers

1. Why does Valli want to ride the bus?
Answer: She is curious about the outside world and becomes fascinated by the bus that regularly passes her village.
2. How does Valli prepare for the journey?
Answer: She carefully observes the bus, learns its fare and timing, saves money and plans the trip without telling the adults.
3. Why does Valli refuse the conductor’s offer of help?
Answer: She wants to prove that she is independent and capable of managing the journey herself.
4. What changes Valli’s mood on the return journey?
Answer: She sees the dead cow that she had earlier found amusing. This makes her realise that life can suddenly change and makes her serious.
5. What qualities does Valli show?
Answer: She shows curiosity, confidence, determination, careful planning and a growing understanding of life.

Poem — The Tale of Custard the Dragon

1. Who proves to be truly brave in the poem?
Answer: Custard the dragon proves to be the truly brave one because he faces the pirate while the others run away.
2. What is the humorous idea in the poem?
Answer: The poem humorously contrasts the characters’ claims of bravery with their actual behaviour when danger appears.
