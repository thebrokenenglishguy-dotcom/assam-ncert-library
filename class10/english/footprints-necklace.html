Questions & Answers

The course of the Loisels’ life changed due to the necklace. Comment.
Answer: After Matilda lost the necklace, the Loisels borrowed a large amount of money to replace it. They spent ten years working hard and repaying the debt, changing their comfortable life into one of poverty and struggle.
What was the cause of Matilda’s ruin? How could she have avoided it?
Answer: Her desire for wealth and social status, followed by the loss of the necklace and failure to tell the truth, caused her ruin. She could have avoided the hardship by telling her friend about the loss immediately.
What would have happened to Matilda if she had confessed to her friend that she had lost the necklace?
Answer: Her friend might have told her that the necklace was not very valuable. The Loisels could then have avoided years of debt and suffering.
If you were caught in a situation like this, how would you have dealt with it?
Answer: I would tell the truth immediately and ask for help. Honest communication would be better than hiding the problem and creating a much larger one.
