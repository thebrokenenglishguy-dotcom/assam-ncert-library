I. A Baker from Goa

1. Why is bread important in Goan life?
Answer: Bread is connected with daily life, festivals, marriages and other social occasions. Traditional bakers therefore have an important place in the community.
2. What did the baker traditionally wear?
Answer: The traditional baker wore a special one-piece dress called the kabai. Later, bakers wore a shirt with short trousers.
3. Why was the baker’s profession profitable?
Answer: Bread was widely used and the baker supplied it regularly. Bakers and their families were generally prosperous.

II. Coorg

1. What makes Coorg special?
Answer: Coorg is famous for its beautiful forests, coffee plantations, wildlife, pleasant climate and brave people.
2. What is the Kodavu community known for?
Answer: The Kodavus are known for their bravery, martial tradition and distinctive culture.

III. Tea from Assam

1. What does Rajvir learn about tea in Assam?
Answer: He learns about the large tea gardens, tea-plucking and the legends connected with the origin of tea.
2. Why are the tea gardens of Assam important?
Answer: Assam is one of the major tea-producing regions and its tea gardens are an important part of its economy and landscape.

Poem — The Trees

1. What do the moving trees suggest?
Answer: They suggest a strong desire for freedom. The trees are imagined as moving out of the house and back towards nature.
