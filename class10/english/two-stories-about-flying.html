Chapter 3 — Two Stories about Flying

His First Flight & The Black Aeroplane

← Back to Library

I. His First Flight

1. Why is the young seagull afraid to fly?
Answer: He is afraid that his wings will not support him and that he will fall into the sea.
2. What finally makes him take his first flight?
Answer: Hunger becomes stronger than his fear. His mother tempts him with food, so he jumps forward and discovers that he can fly.
3. What lesson does the seagull learn?
Answer: He learns that courage and confidence can overcome fear and that trying is necessary for success.

II. The Black Aeroplane

1. Why does the pilot enter the storm?
Answer: He wants to reach home and does not have enough fuel to take a long route around the storm.
2. What problems does he face inside the storm?
Answer: His compass and other instruments stop working. His radio also stops working, and he cannot see where he is going.
3. Who helps him reach the runway?
Answer: A mysterious black aeroplane appears and guides him towards safety. The story deliberately leaves the identity of the helper mysterious.

Poem — How to Tell Wild Animals

1. How does the poet make the poem humorous?
Answer: She gives dangerous animals funny identifying signs and uses playful language and rhyme.

Poem — The Ball Poem

1. What does the lost ball teach the boy?
Answer: It teaches him about loss, responsibility and the fact that some things cannot simply be replaced with money.
