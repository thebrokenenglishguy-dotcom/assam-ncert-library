Questions & Answers

How did the invisible man first become visible?
Answer: Griffin became visible when he stepped in mud. Although his body was invisible, the mud stuck to his feet and showed footprints.
Why was Griffin wandering the streets?
Answer: Griffin had become invisible after using special drugs. After setting fire to his landlord’s house, he had removed his clothes to escape unseen and became a homeless wanderer without money or clothes.
“Griffin was rather a lawless person.” Comment.
Answer: Griffin was a brilliant scientist, but he misused his discovery. He set fire to his landlord’s house, stole clothes and money, and behaved violently. His scientific ability was not matched by good moral behaviour.
How would you assess Griffin as a scientist?
Answer: Griffin was highly intelligent and persistent because he discovered a way to make the human body transparent. However, he lacked responsibility and used his discovery for selfish and harmful purposes.
