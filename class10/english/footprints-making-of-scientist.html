Questions & Answers

How can one become a scientist, an economist, a historian...? Does it simply involve reading many books?
Answer: No. Reading is important, but scientific and other serious work also requires curiosity, observation, thinking, experimentation and a willingness to investigate questions.
How did Richard Ebright’s work lead to important scientific ideas?
Answer: His experiments on monarch butterflies led him to study the hormone from the gold spots on the pupa. His later work contributed to his ideas about how cells read the blueprint of DNA.
What qualities helped Richard Ebright become a successful scientist?
Answer: Curiosity, hard work, careful observation, competition, experimentation and the guidance of his mother and teachers helped him.
