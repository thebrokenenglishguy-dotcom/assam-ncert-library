Questions & Answers

Did you begin to suspect that the lady was not the person Horace Danby took her to be? If so, when?
Answer: The suspicion arises when the woman behaves too confidently and claims to be the lady of the house. Her calm control over Horace and her knowledge of the house suggest that she is deceiving him.
What are the subtle ways in which the lady deceives Horace? Why does he not suspect her?
Answer: She speaks confidently, acts as though she owns the house and gives Horace a convincing reason for opening the safe. Horace does not suspect her because he is frightened and wants to escape quickly.
Why is the description “good and respectable — but not completely honest” apt for Horace?
Answer: Horace behaves politely in ordinary life and has a respectable business, but he secretly steals every year to buy rare books. Therefore he is respectable in some ways but dishonest in another.
Horace was a meticulous planner but still faltered. Where did he go wrong?
Answer: He trusted the woman too easily and allowed her to manipulate him. He broke his own careful plan because he believed her false story.
