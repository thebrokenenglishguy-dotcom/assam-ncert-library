Questions & Answers

1. Whom does Lencho trust completely, and why?
Answer: Lencho has complete faith in God. He believes God sees everything and will help him after the hailstorm destroys his crop.
2. Why does the postmaster decide to help Lencho?
Answer: He is deeply impressed by Lencho’s faith. He wants to protect that faith, so he collects money and sends it to Lencho.
3. Why does Lencho believe that the money came from God?
Answer: Lencho had asked God for help and then received money. His strong faith makes him believe God sent it.
4. Why does Lencho become angry after receiving the money?
Answer: He had asked for 100 pesos but received only 70. He thinks God could not have made a mistake, so he suspects the post-office employees.
5. What is the main irony of the story?
Answer: The post-office workers help Lencho, but Lencho calls them crooks. The people who help him are the very people he distrusts.

Poem — Dust of Snow

1. What changes the poet’s mood?
Answer: A small amount of snow falling from a hemlock tree changes his sad mood and makes the day better.
2. What does the poem show about nature?
Answer: Even a small and ordinary event in nature can bring hope and change a person’s mood.

Poem — Fire and Ice

1. What do fire and ice represent?
Answer: Fire represents desire and uncontrolled passion. Ice represents hatred, coldness and lack of feeling.
2. What is the rhyme scheme?
Answer: The poem mainly follows the rhyme pattern ABAABCBCB, helping the contrasting ideas sound connected.
