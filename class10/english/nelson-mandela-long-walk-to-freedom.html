Questions & Answers

1. Why was the inauguration an important event?
Answer: It marked the end of apartheid rule and the beginning of a democratic South Africa in which people of all races had rights.
2. What does Mandela mean by his ‘twin obligations’?
Answer: He means his duties towards his family and his duties towards his people and country.
3. How did Mandela’s idea of freedom change?
Answer: As a child he saw freedom as personal freedom. With age he understood that true freedom also means freedom and dignity for all people.
4. What changed Mandela from a frightened person into a determined fighter?
Answer: His desire for the freedom of his people became stronger than his fear. This hunger for freedom changed his life.
5. What ideals does Mandela express for South Africa?
Answer: He wants a country free from racial oppression, where all people live with dignity, equality, peace and freedom.

Poem — A Tiger in the Zoo

1. How is the tiger’s life in the cage different from its natural life?
Answer: In the cage the tiger is restricted and helpless. In the forest it would move freely, hunt and live according to its natural instincts.
2. What feeling does the poem create?
Answer: It creates sympathy for the caged tiger and shows the cruelty of taking wild animals away from their natural freedom.
