Questions & Answers

Bholi had many apprehensions about going to school. What made her feel that she was going to a better place than her home?
Answer: At school, the teacher spoke to her kindly and encouraged her. The classroom also had colourful pictures and girls of her age. She began to feel safe and hopeful.
How did Bholi’s teacher play an important role in changing the course of her life?
Answer: The teacher treated Bholi with patience and affection. She encouraged her to speak, learn and overcome fear. This education gave Bholi confidence and independence.
Why did Bholi first agree to an unequal match? Why did she later reject the marriage?
Answer: At first she believed that she might not get another chance to marry because of her appearance and stammering. She rejected the marriage when Bishamber demanded money and insulted her. By then education had given her confidence and self-respect.
Why is Bholi called Sulekha again near the end of the story?
Answer: Sulekha is her real name. The use of her real name suggests that she has regained her identity and confidence instead of remaining the timid Bholi whom others had looked down upon.
