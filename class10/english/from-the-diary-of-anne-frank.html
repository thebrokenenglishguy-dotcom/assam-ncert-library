Questions & Answers

1. Why does Anne begin writing a diary?
Answer: She feels that she does not have a true friend with whom she can share her private thoughts. She decides to treat her diary as a friend named Kitty.
2. Why does Anne give a brief sketch of her family?
Answer: She wants Kitty to understand her background and the people who are important in her life.
3. What does Anne’s first essay say about her habit of talking?
Answer: She argues humorously that talking is a student’s natural habit and that she inherited the trait from her mother.
4. Why is Mr Keesing annoyed with Anne?
Answer: He thinks Anne talks too much during class. He gives her extra writing assignments as punishment.
5. How does Anne finally change Mr Keesing’s attitude?
Answer: Her humorous essays make him laugh and understand her personality. He eventually stops giving her extra homework and even jokes with her.

Poem — Amanda!

1. What does Amanda want?
Answer: She wants freedom from constant instructions and dreams of a peaceful life where she can be herself.
2. What contrast is shown in the poem?
Answer: The poem contrasts the adult’s repeated instructions with Amanda’s private dreams and desire for independence.
