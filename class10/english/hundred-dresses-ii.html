Questions & Answers

1. What does Mr Petronski’s letter tell the class?
Answer: It says that Wanda and her family have moved to another place because they are tired of people making fun of their name and background.
2. How does Miss Mason react to the letter?
Answer: She is unhappy and disturbed. She tells the class that making fun of someone because of their name or nationality is wrong.
3. How does Maddie feel after hearing the letter?
Answer: She feels guilty because she had remained silent while Wanda was teased. She wishes she had defended Wanda.
4. What do Wanda’s drawings reveal about her?
Answer: They reveal her creativity and talent. Her drawings also show the imagination behind her claim about the hundred dresses.
5. What does Maddie decide to do?
Answer: She decides to find Wanda and tell her that she and Peggy are sorry and that they liked her drawings.

Poem — Animals

1. Why does the poet admire animals?
Answer: He feels animals are calm, self-contained and free from many human weaknesses such as greed, dissatisfaction and guilt.
2. What does the poet find troubling about human beings?
Answer: He feels humans have become restless, greedy and overly concerned with possessions and social status.
