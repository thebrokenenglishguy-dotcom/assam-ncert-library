Questions & Answers

1. Why does Maxwell decide to keep an otter?
Answer: After losing his dog, he wants a new companion. He becomes interested in otters and decides to keep one.
2. What kind of animal is Mijbil?
Answer: Mij is an intelligent, playful and affectionate otter. He enjoys water, games and exploring his surroundings.
3. Why do people in London find Mij unusual?
Answer: They are unfamiliar with otters and often make strange guesses about what kind of animal he is.
4. What does Mij’s behaviour show about him?
Answer: His playful habits, curiosity and attachment to Maxwell show that he is intelligent, friendly and full of energy.
5. Why is the story called Mijbil the Otter?
Answer: Mijbil is the central character, and the story describes Maxwell’s close relationship with this unusual pet.

Poem — Fog

1. How does the poet describe fog?
Answer: The fog is compared to a cat because it comes quietly, sits over the city and then moves away silently.
