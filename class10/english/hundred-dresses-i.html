Questions & Answers

1. How is Wanda different from the other children?
Answer: She is poor, wears the same faded dress and lives at Boggins Heights. Her unusual name also makes her stand out.
2. Why does Wanda say she has a hundred dresses?
Answer: She is being teased about her clothes. Her claim of having a hundred beautiful dresses is connected with her imagination and her wish to respond to the teasing.
3. Why does Maddie feel uncomfortable when Peggy teases Wanda?
Answer: Maddie is also poor and wears hand-me-down clothes. She fears that she could become the next target, and she knows the teasing is wrong.
4. Why does Maddie remain silent?
Answer: She lacks the courage to oppose Peggy because Peggy is popular and Maddie is afraid of becoming a target herself.
5. What important issue does the story raise?
Answer: It raises the issue of bullying, poverty, prejudice and the need to stand up against unfair treatment.
