Questions & Answers

1. Why does Lomov visit Chubukov’s house?
Answer: He comes to ask for Natalya’s hand in marriage.
2. Why does Lomov and Natalya’s conversation become an argument?
Answer: Instead of discussing marriage calmly, they begin quarrelling over the ownership of Oxen Meadows and later over their dogs.
3. What does the play show about Lomov’s character?
Answer: He is nervous, argumentative and easily upset. He wants marriage but repeatedly loses control during the discussion.
4. Why is the play humorous?
Answer: The serious purpose of a marriage proposal is repeatedly interrupted by silly arguments, exaggerated reactions and misunderstandings.
5. What is the main message of the play?
Answer: The play humorously shows how greed, pride and unnecessary arguments can make simple matters complicated.
