Complete syllabus — all five units

Select any unit to expand its topics.

Unit titles and topic summaries below follow the Semester IV syllabus document available in the study library.

Unit I — Introduction to Pharmaceutical Biotechnology

Biotechnology in pharmaceutical sciences; protein therapeutics and analytical characterization of proteins; monoclonal antibodies; antigens; enzyme immobilization; cell culture and immobilization techniques; pharmaceutical applications.

Unit II — Genetic Engineering and Recombinant DNA Technology

Principles of genetic engineering and recombinant DNA technology; production of interferons, hepatitis-B vaccine and insulin; polymerase chain reaction (PCR) and its applications; mutations and types of mutants.

Unit III — Microbial Biotransformation and Fermentation Technology

Microbial biotransformation; fermentation principles and process control; industrial production of alcohol, penicillins, citric acid and vitamin B12; collection, processing and storage of blood products.

Unit IV — Gene Therapy, Genomics and Personalized Medicine

Types and methodologies of gene therapy; delivery systems; ethical concerns and challenges; Human Genome Project; role of bioinformatics and artificial intelligence in personalized medicine.

Unit V — Immunology, Vaccines and Sera

Basics of immunology; types and generations of vaccines; immunization strategies; vaccine preparation, evaluation and standardization; regulatory aspects; sera therapy; marketed products; challenges and newer approaches.
