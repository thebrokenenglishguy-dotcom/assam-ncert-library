Complete syllabus — all five units

Select any unit to expand its topics.

Unit titles and topic summaries below follow the Semester IV syllabus document available in the study library.

Unit I — Introduction to Social Pharmacy and Public Health

Definition, scope, historical development and importance of social pharmacy; multidisciplinary nature; public-health concepts, history, core functions and ethics; relationship between social pharmacy and public health; WHO definition and dimensions of health; social, economic, environmental, lifestyle and healthcare determinants; health indicators.

Unit II — Health Systems, Policy and Pharmacoepidemiology

Global healthcare delivery systems; Indian healthcare structure, public/private sectors and primary, secondary and tertiary care; health-policy formulation and India's National Health Policy; National Health Mission including NRHM and NUHM; pharmacoepidemiology; incidence, prevalence, endemic, epidemic, pandemic, morbidity and mortality rates, odds ratio and relative risk; basic biostatistics, data types and presentation.

Unit III — Preventive Healthcare, Health Promotion and Communicable Diseases

Primordial, primary, secondary and tertiary prevention; pharmacist roles in immunization, screening and lifestyle counselling; health education principles and materials; antenatal and postnatal care, breastfeeding and immunization schedules; transmission, prevention and control of tuberculosis, HIV/AIDS, malaria, dengue, typhoid and influenza.

Unit IV — Non-Communicable Diseases, Nutrition, Mental Health and National Programs

Diabetes, hypertension, cardiovascular diseases, chronic respiratory diseases and cancer; balanced diet, macro- and micronutrients, malnutrition, deficiency disorders, food safety and adulteration; common mental disorders, stigma and mental well-being; Indian national health programs for maternal and child health, NCDs, communicable diseases, tobacco control and deafness prevention.

Unit V — Pharmacoeconomics, Rational Use of Medicines, Professional Roles and Future Trends

Pharmacoeconomics; cost-benefit, cost-effectiveness and cost-utility analyses; rational use of medicines and irrational-use problems; medication adherence and improvement strategies; alcohol, tobacco, opioid and prescription-drug misuse; professionalism and ethics; disaster management; telepharmacy, digital health, personalized medicine and expanding pharmacist roles.
