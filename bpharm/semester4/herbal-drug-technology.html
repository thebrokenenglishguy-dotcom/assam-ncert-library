Complete syllabus — all five units

Select any unit to expand its topics.

Unit titles and topic summaries below follow the Semester IV syllabus document available in the study library.

Unit I — Plant Tissue Culture and Standardized Extracts

Plant tissue culture as an alternative source of medicine; historical development; types of cultures; nutritional requirements; growth and maintenance of callus and suspension cultures; elicitation, genetic transformation, biotransformation, precursor feeding and somaclonal variation; medicinal-plant biomanufacturing and plant factories; shikonin, paclitaxel and oral vaccines; optimization and TLC fingerprinting of standardized medicinal-plant extracts including Ashwagandha, Shatavari, Licorice, Neem, Haritaki, sweet lime peel, Bacopa, green tea, Tribulus and Vasaka.

Unit II — Herbal Formulations, Excipients and Cosmetics

Conventional formulations: syrups, mixtures, powders, capsules, tablets, creams and ointments; novel dosage forms including phytosomes, liposomes and nanoformulations; composition, preparation and characterization using standardized extracts and bioactives; herbal skincare, hair-care and oral-hygiene products; sunscreen lotion/gel, hair oil, shampoo, herbal dye, mouthwash, chewing gums, candies, gargles, face serums and face packs; natural excipients including colorants, sweeteners, binders, diluents, viscosity builders, disintegrants, flavours, perfumes, protective agents, waxes, bleaching agents and antioxidants.

Unit III — Quality Control and Standardisation of Herbal Medicines

WHO, AYUSH and EU guidelines for quality control, stability and shelf-life studies; standardisation of botanicals and formulations; testing methods and regulatory considerations; DNA fingerprinting and molecular markers including rbcL, matK and SCAR; forensic pharmacognosy and identification of illicit herbal drugs such as Cannabis and Opium.

Unit IV — Herb–Drug, Herb–Food and Herb–Herb Interactions

Role of ADME, cytochrome P450 and P-glycoprotein; St. John’s wort with warfarin; Ginkgo biloba with aspirin; licorice with salty foods; turmeric with fats; green tea with iron-rich foods; ephedra with ginseng; chamomile with valerian; plant- and food-related allergy, intolerance and toxicity.

Unit V — Regulatory Requirements of Herbal Drugs and Botanicals

Indian regulatory framework for herbal and ASU medicines; regulatory bodies; ASU DTAB and ASU DCC; Schedule T GMP requirements; Schedule E1 poisonous drugs under AYUSH; Drugs and Cosmetics Act provisions for herbal/ASU medicines, registration, trade and export; phytopharmaceuticals and Ayush Aahara.
